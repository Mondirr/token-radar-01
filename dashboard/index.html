How the value is worked out

Every protocol below records no token in the sealed DefiLlama snapshot: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no ticker. That is the record, not proof — a protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are kept off the board, as are exchanges and versioned pools: a wrapped token is not a launch waiting to happen.

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × age factor × chain factor. The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in the same category whose token already trades (the global median when a category has fewer than three). Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped. Age is how long DefiLlama has tracked the protocol. Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: the same formula scores every row, from the snapshot's own figures.

Implied values are this radar's own estimates, not a prediction that a token will be issued or what it would trade at. This page is not financial advice.
